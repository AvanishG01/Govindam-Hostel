About Govindam

A residence run like a home, managed like a business.

Govindam Residences was founded on a simple observation: most hostels are built for landlords, not for the young women who live in them, or the parents who worry about them. We designed every floor plan, meal schedule and security protocol around those two people instead.

What started as a single 40-bed property has grown into a residence trusted by families from across the region — without losing the part that mattered: someone always answers the phone, and someone is always on the floor.

  • Female-only staff on every shift, including night security
  • Parents get a direct line to the warden, not a call centre
  • Rooms inspected weekly for hygiene and maintenance
