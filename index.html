About us

Run like a home, managed like a business.

Started in 2016 by a family that once struggled to find a safe hostel for their own daughter, Shivalik Residency now houses over 120 students across two buildings. Every room is cleaned weekly, every gate is logged, and every meal is cooked on-site by our resident cook — not outsourced, not reheated.

  • Verified entry & exit log — parents can call the warden any time for an update.
  • In-house mess — fixed, home-style menu with no hidden charges.
  • Warden on every floor — a real adult on-site 24×7, not just a guard at the gate.
  • 10-minute campus radius — no long commutes eating into study time.
